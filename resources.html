Partners and institutions

Institute Nexus for Quantum Technologies NexQT, the Nexus for Quantum Technologies, is uOttawa’s cross-faculty quantum institute: more than 80 researchers in science, engineering, medicine and law working on quantum sensing, communication, computing, materials and photonics.
