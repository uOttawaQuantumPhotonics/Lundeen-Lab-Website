Principal investigator

Jeff Lundeen

Associate Professor of Physics, University of Ottawa · Director, Nexus for Quantum Technologies (NexQT)

Jeff Lundeen is an experimental quantum physicist working in quantum photonics, quantum optics, quantum information and measurement, and nonlinear and structured light. He was the Canada Research Chair in Quantum Photonics (Tier 2) from 2014 to 2024, and is a Fellow of the Max Planck – uOttawa Centre for Extreme and Quantum Photonics and of the NRC–uOttawa Joint Centre for Extreme Photonics.

Since 2025 Jeff has directed NexQT, the Nexus for Quantum Technologies: uOttawa’s cross-faculty quantum institute. It brings together more than 80 researchers from science, engineering, medicine and law to work on quantum sensing, communication, computing, materials and photonics.

He grew up in Toronto, did his undergraduate degree at Queen’s University, and earned his MSc and PhD at the University of Toronto with Aephraim Steinberg. As a postdoctoral researcher he worked with Ian Walmsley at the Clarendon Laboratory, University of Oxford, then briefly at ICFO in Barcelona with Morgan Mitchell. From 2009 to 2013 he was a research officer at the National Research Council Canada, where he performed the first direct measurement of the quantum wavefunction. He joined uOttawa in 2013.
